Block Trio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Developer: TaraPDF (seller on the App Store: Kaan Cabukoglu) · Contact: kaanyakuphancabukoglu@gmail.com

Block Trio is a puzzle game for all ages: put three blocks of the same color in one tray to clear it. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. We keep it to the minimum the game needs.

What we do not collect

  • No account to create and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or phone number. The Weekly League and the Weekend Cup use an anonymous player ID that the game creates by itself (see Weekly League and Weekend Cup).
  • No precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. The services below may estimate your approximate location (country or region) from your IP address.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, season pass, settings) is stored only on your device. The one exception is your Weekly League and Weekend Cup results (see Weekly League and Weekend Cup).

Age question

When you first open the game we ask for your year of birth. The answer stays on your device and is used only to choose the right kind of ads: players under 13 get child-directed ads, and players under 16 get non-personalized ads. We do not send your year of birth to us.

Data used by services in the app

Game analytics (Google Firebase Analytics). We count game events such as starting or finishing a level, number of moves, boosters used and ads shown. Firebase also records basic device information (model, OS version, language, approximate country from IP address) and an app instance ID. It also records in-app purchase events (which item and its price). We use this to see which levels are too hard, which items are bought and whether players come back, not to identify you. For players under 13, analytics is limited to what is needed to run the game.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). If the app crashes, a report with the error, device model and OS version is sent so we can fix it.

Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config). The app downloads a few settings (for example ad frequency limits) so we can adjust them without an update. These settings never change level difficulty for individual players.

Ads (Google AdMob). The free version shows ads: none in the first 8 levels, then at most one every few levels, only between levels and never during a level, plus optional ads you choose to watch for rewards. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and information about ad interactions to show and measure ads. For players under 13 ads are child-directed and not personalized; for players under 16 they are not personalized. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland adults are asked for consent first and can change their choice any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. On iOS, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking; we do not ask players under 16. Any purchase removes in-between ads.

Purchases (Google Play / Apple App Store). Coins, booster packs, the season pass, ad removal and the optional monthly club are handled by the store. Prices are the same for everyone. To check that a purchase is genuine, the store's receipt is sent to our verification service and is not stored; we never see your payment details. Parents can require approval for purchases in Google Play Family settings or with Ask to Buy on Apple devices.

Weekly League and Weekend Cup

Block Trio has two online competitions: the Weekly League (a weekly ranking in groups of up to 30 players) and the Weekend Cup (a weekend event once a month). They need a server, so a small amount of data leaves your device. If neither is switched on in your game, none of this is collected.

  • An anonymous player ID. The game gets a random ID from Google Firebase Authentication by itself, without asking you: while the Weekly League is switched on, as soon as the map screen loads the league; with only the Weekend Cup switched on, the first time you open the Weekend Cup screen. There is no sign-up, password, email or name, and the ID is not your device ID or advertising ID. Its only job is to keep your board name, points and rewards together on the server and to limit how often scores can be sent.
  • A board name. The server picks it from the game's word list: a describing word, an animal or gemstone, and a number (for example "Swift Tiger 42"). You cannot type your own text. You can hide it at any time (other players then see "Hidden player") and get a new one up to 3 times.
  • Your results. When you win a level or the Daily Puzzle with new stars (Weekly League), or solve a Weekend Cup level, the game sends the list of moves and boosters you used. The server replays them and works out your stars or score itself, which keeps the board fair. The list is not saved. What is saved: your best stars for each level, your best Weekend Cup scores, your points and when you reached them, your rank, your league and cups, and any result or reward waiting for you.

Why. Only to run the competitions: to show the ranking, give rewards and stop cheating (for example by limiting how often scores can be sent). This data is not used for advertising, and we do not link it to your advertising ID or analytics data. Other players see your board name (or "Hidden player") and your points, never your player ID.

Where. With Google Firebase, which processes the data for us. The game server (Cloud Functions) and the database (Cloud Firestore) are in the EU (region europe-west1, Belgium). The service that issues the anonymous ID (Firebase Authentication) runs in the United States. We do not save your IP address with your player record; Google's servers use it to deliver the request and protect the service, and keep it in technical logs for a few weeks.

How long. Your player record and results are kept until you delete them (see Delete your data); nothing expires by itself. The final board of a finished Weekend Cup is kept so that results and rewards can be shown; your row in it stays linked to your player ID until you delete your data. When a league week ends, player IDs are removed from that week's table; it then holds only board names and points, and it is kept.

Children. Players under 13 can take part, and the anonymous player ID is created for them in the same way. Their board name is always hidden and cannot be made visible; others only see "Hidden player". The year of birth is not sent: the game only tells the server to keep the name hidden. There is no chat and no free text for anyone.

Children

Block Trio can be played by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The one exception is the Weekly League and the Weekend Cup: there an anonymous player ID and game results are stored as described above, and the board name of a player under 13 is always hidden. Children receive child-directed, non-personalized ads, and no tracking permission is requested from them.

Your choices

  • Ad consent: Settings → Privacy & ad choices (where available).
  • Advertising ID: reset or limit it in your device settings.
  • Board name: hide it or get a new one (up to 3 times) on the Weekly League or Weekend Cup screen.
  • Weekly League and Weekend Cup data: delete it in the game with Settings → Delete my ranking data (see Delete your data).
  • Uninstalling the app removes all game data stored on your device.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months. To ask about or delete data linked to your device, email us.

Delete your data (Block Trio by TaraPDF)

  1. Game data on your phone (levels, stars, coins, settings, year of birth): uninstall Block Trio, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Block Trio → Storage. On iPhone and iPad, delete the app. It is deleted at once.
  2. Data held by Google for the app (analytics events, crash reports, app instance ID): email kaanyakuphancabukoglu@gmail.com with the subject "Block Trio data deletion". We delete the analytics data linked to your app instance within 30 days and confirm by email.
  3. Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. On iPhone and iPad, turn off tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  4. Weekly League and Weekend Cup data (anonymous player ID, board name, stars, points, rank and rewards): in the game, open Settings → Delete my ranking data and confirm. Your player record (board name, stars, league, cups and waiting results), your row in this week's league table, and your Weekend Cup scores and board rows are deleted from our server at once. The game then deletes the anonymous ID too (if that step fails, it signs out so the ID is no longer used) and clears the league and cup data saved on your device. Your coins and level progress on the device stay. Not removed: tables of earlier league weeks; once a week is closed they hold only a board name and points, with no player ID. Deleting does not switch the competitions off: if they are still on in your game, it starts again with a new ID and a new board name. The row is shown in Settings only while the Weekly League or the Weekend Cup is switched on. If you do not see it, or no longer have the game, email kaanyakuphancabukoglu@gmail.com with the subject "Block Trio data deletion" and tell us your board name (shown on the Weekly League or Weekend Cup screen) and roughly your points; we delete your player record, your board rows and your anonymous ID within 30 days and confirm by email. Uninstalling the game does not delete this server data. To limit abuse after a deletion, the anonymous ID is kept together with the number of deletions and the hourly submission counter for 24 hours; then this record is deleted automatically.

Kept: purchase records stay with Google Play or Apple as required by law; we do not hold them. Without a request, analytics data is deleted automatically after 14 months and crash reports after 90 days.

Block Trio — Gizlilik Politikası

Block Trio her yaşa uygun bir bulmaca oyunudur. Hesap ve giriş yoktur; adını, e-postanı ya da telefonunu istemeyiz. Kesin (GPS) konum, rehber, fotoğraf, kamera ve mikrofon kullanılmaz; aşağıdaki servisler IP adresinden yaklaşık ülke ya da bölge çıkarabilir. İlerlemen (seviyeler, yıldızlar, jeton, güçlendiriciler, sezon yolu, ayarlar) yalnız cihazında saklanır; tek istisna Haftalık Lig ve Hafta Sonu Kupası sonuçlarıdır (aşağıda).

Yaş sorusu: İlk açılışta doğum yılın sorulur. Cevap yalnız cihazında kalır ve yalnız reklam türünü seçmek için kullanılır: 13 yaş altına çocuğa yönelik, 16 yaş altına kişiselleştirilmemiş reklam gösterilir. Doğum yılın bize gönderilmez.

Kullanılan servisler: Firebase Analytics (seviye başlama/bitirme, hamle, güçlendirici ve reklam sayıları, satın alma olayları (ürün ve fiyat); cihaz modeli, sürüm, dil, IP'den yaklaşık ülke), Crashlytics (çökme raporları), Remote Config (reklam sıklığı gibi uzaktan ayarlar; seviye zorluğunu kişiye göre değiştirmez), Google AdMob (reklam; reklam kimliği; AB/İngiltere/İsviçre'de yetişkinlere önce onay sorulur, Ayarlar → Gizlilik ve reklam seçimleri'nden değiştirilir), mağaza üzerinden satın alma (fiyatlar herkese aynıdır, ödeme bilgini görmeyiz; ebeveynler Google Play Aile ayarlarından ya da Apple'da Satın Almak İçin Sor ile onay zorunlu kılabilir). iPhone ve iPad'de reklam kişiselleştirmesi yalnız izleme izni verirsen açılır; 16 yaş altına bu izin sorulmaz.

Haftalık Lig ve Hafta Sonu Kupası: Block Trio'da iki çevrimiçi yarışma vardır: Haftalık Lig (en çok 30 kişilik gruplarda haftalık sıralama) ve Hafta Sonu Kupası (ayda bir hafta sonu etkinliği). Sunucu gerektirdikleri için az miktarda veri cihazından çıkar; ikisi de oyununda açık değilse aşağıdakilerin hiçbiri toplanmaz. Oyun, sana sormadan, Google Firebase Authentication'dan rastgele bir anonim oyuncu kimliği alır: Haftalık Lig açıkken harita ekranı ligi yüklediği anda; yalnız Hafta Sonu Kupası açıkken kupa ekranını ilk açtığında. Kayıt, şifre, e-posta ya da ad yoktur; bu kimlik cihaz kimliğin ya da reklam kimliğin değildir. Tek işi tablo adını, puanlarını ve ödüllerini sunucuda bir arada tutmak ve puan gönderme sıklığını sınırlamaktır. Sunucu sana oyunun kelime listesinden bir tablo adı seçer: bir sıfat, bir hayvan ya da değerli taş ve bir sayı (örnek: "Hızlı Kaplan 42"). Kendi yazını yazamazsın; adını istediğin an gizleyebilir (başkaları "Gizli oyuncu" görür) ve en çok 3 kez yenisini alabilirsin. Bir bölümü ya da Günün Bulmacası'nı yeni yıldızla kazandığında (Haftalık Lig) ya da bir Hafta Sonu Kupası bölümünü çözdüğünde oyun, yaptığın hamlelerin ve kullandığın güçlendiricilerin listesini gönderir; sunucu yıldızını ya da puanını bunları yeniden oynatarak kendisi hesaplar ve listeyi saklamaz. Saklananlar: her bölümdeki en iyi yıldızın, Hafta Sonu Kupası'ndaki en iyi puanların, puanların ve onlara ulaştığın an, sıran, ligin ve kupaların, bekleyen sonucun ya da ödülün. Bu veri yalnız yarışmaları yürütmek içindir (sıralama, ödül, hile önleme); reklam için kullanılmaz, reklam kimliğinle ya da analiz verinle birleştirilmez. Diğer oyuncular yalnız tablo adını (ya da "Gizli oyuncu") ve puanını görür, oyuncu kimliğini görmez.

Bu veri, bizim adımıza işleyen Google Firebase'de durur: oyun sunucusu (Cloud Functions) ve veritabanı (Cloud Firestore) AB'dedir (europe-west1 bölgesi, Belçika); anonim kimliği veren servis (Firebase Authentication) ABD'de çalışır. IP adresini oyuncu kaydınla birlikte saklamayız; Google'ın sunucuları onu isteği iletmek ve servisi korumak için kullanır, teknik kayıtlarda birkaç hafta tutar. Oyuncu kaydın ve sonuçların, sen silene kadar tutulur (aşağıda Verini silme); kendiliğinden silinen bir şey yoktur. Biten Hafta Sonu Kupası'nın son tablosu, sonuç ve ödül gösterilebilsin diye saklanır; oradaki satırın, verini silene kadar oyuncu kimliğine bağlı kalır. Lig haftası bitince o haftanın tablosundan oyuncu kimlikleri silinir; tabloda yalnız tablo adları ve puanlar kalır ve tablo saklanır. 13 yaş altındaki oyuncular da katılabilir ve anonim oyuncu kimliği onlar için de aynı şekilde oluşur. Onların tablo adı her zaman gizlidir ve görünür yapılamaz (başkaları yalnız "Gizli oyuncu" görür); doğum yılı gönderilmez, sunucuya yalnız "adı gizle" bilgisi gider. Sohbet ve serbest yazı kimse için yoktur.
