Block Trio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Developer: TaraPDF (seller on the App Store: Kaan Cabukoglu) · Contact: kaanyakuphancabukoglu@gmail.com

Block Trio is a puzzle game for all ages: put three blocks of the same color in one tray to clear it. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. We keep it to the minimum the game needs.

What we do not collect

  • No account to create and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or phone number. Leaderboards use an anonymous player ID that the game creates by itself (see Leaderboards and events).
  • No precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. The services below may estimate your approximate location (country or region) from your IP address.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, season pass, settings) is stored only on your device. The one exception is your leaderboard results (see Leaderboards and events).

Age question

When you first open the game we ask for your year of birth. The answer stays on your device and is used only to choose the right kind of ads: players under 13 get child-directed ads, and players under 16 get non-personalized ads. We do not send your year of birth to us.

Data used by services in the app

Game analytics (Google Firebase Analytics). We count game events such as starting or finishing a level, number of moves, boosters used and ads shown. Firebase also records basic device information (model, OS version, language, approximate country from IP address) and an app instance ID. It also records in-app purchase events (which item and its price). We use this to see which levels are too hard, which items are bought and whether players come back, not to identify you. For players under 13, analytics is limited to what is needed to run the game.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). If the app crashes, a report with the error, device model and OS version is sent so we can fix it.

Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config). The app downloads a few settings (for example ad frequency limits) so we can adjust them without an update. These settings never change level difficulty for individual players.

Ads (Google AdMob). The free version shows ads: none in the first 8 levels, then at most one every few levels, only between levels and never during a level, plus optional ads you choose to watch for rewards. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and information about ad interactions to show and measure ads. For players under 13 ads are child-directed and not personalized; for players under 16 they are not personalized. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland adults are asked for consent first and can change their choice any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. On iOS, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking; we do not ask players under 16. Any purchase removes in-between ads.

Purchases (Google Play / Apple App Store). Coins, booster packs, the season pass, ad removal and the optional monthly club are handled by the store. Prices are the same for everyone. To check that a purchase is genuine, the store's receipt is sent to our verification service and is not stored; we never see your payment details. Parents can require approval for purchases in Google Play Family settings or with Ask to Buy on Apple devices.

Leaderboards and events

Block Trio has competitions you can take part in: the Weekend Cup (a weekend event once a month) and the Weekly League. They only work online, so a small amount of data leaves your device. If these features are not switched on in your game, none of this is collected.

  • An anonymous player ID. The first time the game connects to the leaderboard server it gets a random ID from Google Firebase Authentication. There is no sign-up, password, email or name, and the ID is not your device ID or advertising ID.
  • A board name. The server picks it from the game's word list (for example "Swift Tiger 42"). You cannot type your own text. You can hide it at any time; other players then see "Hidden player".
  • Your results. When you solve a level that counts, the game sends the list of moves you made. The server replays them and works out your score or stars itself, which keeps the board fair. The move list is not saved. What is saved: your best scores and stars, when you reached them, your rank, your league tier and cups, and any reward waiting for you.

Why. Only to run the competitions: to show the ranking, give rewards and stop cheating (for example by limiting how often scores can be sent). This data is not used for advertising, and we do not link it to your advertising ID or analytics data. Other players see your board name (or "Hidden player") and your score, never your player ID.

Where. With Google Firebase, which processes the data for us. The game server (Cloud Functions) and the database (Cloud Firestore) are in the EU (region europe-west1, Belgium). The service that issues the anonymous ID (Firebase Authentication) runs in the United States. We do not save your IP address with your player record; Google's servers use it to deliver the request and protect the service, and keep it in technical logs for a few weeks.

How long. Your player record and results are kept until you ask us to delete them (see Delete your data). The final board of a finished event or league week is kept so that results and rewards can be shown.

Children. For players under 13 the board name is always hidden and cannot be made visible; others only see "Hidden player". The year of birth is not sent: the game only tells the server to keep the name hidden. There is no chat and no free text for anyone.

Children

Block Trio can be played by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The one exception is leaderboards and events: there an anonymous player ID and game results are stored as described above, and the board name of a player under 13 is always hidden. Children receive child-directed, non-personalized ads, and no tracking permission is requested from them.

Your choices

  • Ad consent: Settings → Privacy & ad choices (where available).
  • Advertising ID: reset or limit it in your device settings.
  • Leaderboard name: hide it or get a new one (up to 3 times) in the leaderboard screen.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all game data stored on your device.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months. To ask about or delete data linked to your device, email us.

Delete your data (Block Trio by TaraPDF)

  1. Game data on your phone (levels, stars, coins, settings, year of birth): uninstall Block Trio, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Block Trio → Storage. On iPhone and iPad, delete the app. It is deleted at once.
  2. Data held by Google for the app (analytics events, crash reports, app instance ID): email kaanyakuphancabukoglu@gmail.com with the subject "Block Trio data deletion". We delete the analytics data linked to your app instance within 30 days and confirm by email.
  3. Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. On iPhone and iPad, turn off tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  4. Leaderboard data (anonymous player ID, board name, scores, rank and rewards): email kaanyakuphancabukoglu@gmail.com with the subject "Block Trio data deletion" and tell us your board name (shown in the game's leaderboard screen) and roughly your score. We delete your player record, your board entries and your anonymous ID within 30 days and confirm by email. Uninstalling the game does not delete this server data.

Kept: purchase records stay with Google Play or Apple as required by law; we do not hold them. Without a request, analytics data is deleted automatically after 14 months and crash reports after 90 days.

Block Trio — Gizlilik Politikası

Block Trio her yaşa uygun bir bulmaca oyunudur. Hesap ve giriş yoktur; adını, e-postanı ya da telefonunu istemeyiz. İlerlemen (seviyeler, yıldızlar, jeton, güçlendiriciler, sezon yolu, ayarlar) yalnız cihazında saklanır; tek istisna sıralama sonuçlarıdır (aşağıda).

Yaş sorusu: İlk açılışta doğum yılın sorulur. Cevap yalnız cihazında kalır ve yalnız reklam türünü seçmek için kullanılır: 13 yaş altına çocuğa yönelik, 16 yaş altına kişiselleştirilmemiş reklam gösterilir. Doğum yılın bize gönderilmez.

Kullanılan servisler: Firebase Analytics (seviye başlama/bitirme, hamle, güçlendirici ve reklam sayıları, satın alma olayları (ürün ve fiyat); cihaz modeli, sürüm, dil, IP'den yaklaşık ülke), Crashlytics (çökme raporları), Remote Config (reklam sıklığı gibi uzaktan ayarlar; seviye zorluğunu kişiye göre değiştirmez), Google AdMob (reklam; reklam kimliği; AB/İngiltere/İsviçre'de yetişkinlere önce onay sorulur, Ayarlar → Gizlilik ve reklam seçimleri'nden değiştirilir), mağaza üzerinden satın alma (fiyatlar herkese aynıdır, ödeme bilgini görmeyiz; ebeveynler Google Play Aile ayarlarından ya da Apple'da Satın Almak İçin Sor ile onay zorunlu kılabilir). iPhone ve iPad'de reklam kişiselleştirmesi yalnız izleme izni verirsen açılır; 16 yaş altına bu izin sorulmaz.

Sıralama ve etkinlikler: Hafta Sonu Kupası (ayda bir hafta sonu etkinliği) ve Haftalık Lig çevrimiçi çalışır; bu özellikler oyununda açık değilse aşağıdakilerin hiçbiri toplanmaz. Oyun sıralama sunucusuna ilk bağlandığında Google Firebase Authentication'dan rastgele bir anonim oyuncu kimliği alır: kayıt, şifre, e-posta ya da ad yoktur; bu kimlik cihaz kimliğin ya da reklam kimliğin değildir. Sunucu sana oyunun kelime listesinden bir tablo adı seçer (örnek: "Hızlı Kaplan 42"); kendi yazını yazamazsın, adını istediğin an gizleyebilirsin (başkaları "Gizli oyuncu" görür). Sayılan bir bölümü çözdüğünde oyun hamle listeni gönderir; sunucu puanını ya da yıldızını bu hamleleri yeniden oynatarak kendisi hesaplar ve hamle listesini saklamaz. Saklananlar: en iyi puan ve yıldızların, bunlara ulaştığın an, sıran, lig kademen ve kupaların, bekleyen ödülün. Bu veri yalnız yarışmaları yürütmek içindir (sıralama, ödül, hile önleme); reklam için kullanılmaz, reklam kimliğinle ya da analiz verinle birleştirilmez. Diğer oyuncular yalnız tablo adını (ya da "Gizli oyuncu") ve puanını görür, oyuncu kimliğini görmez.

Bu veri, bizim adımıza işleyen Google Firebase'de durur: oyun sunucusu (Cloud Functions) ve veritabanı (Cloud Firestore) AB'dedir (europe-west1 bölgesi, Belçika); anonim kimliği veren servis (Firebase Authentication) ABD'de çalışır. IP adresini oyuncu kaydınla birlikte saklamayız; Google'ın sunucuları onu isteği iletmek ve servisi korumak için kullanır, teknik kayıtlarda birkaç hafta tutar. Oyuncu kaydın ve sonuçların, sen silinmesini isteyene kadar tutulur; biten etkinliğin ya da lig haftasının son tablosu, sonuç ve ödül gösterilebilsin diye saklanır. 13 yaş altındaki oyuncuların tablo adı her zaman gizlidir ve görünür yapılamaz; doğum yılı gönderilmez, sunucuya yalnız "adı gizle" bilgisi gider. Sohbet ve serbest yazı kimse için yoktur.

Çocuklar oynayabilir; çocuklardan bilerek kişisel bilgi toplamayız (tek istisna: sıralama için anonim oyuncu kimliği ve oyun sonuçları; 13 yaş altında tablo adı hep gizlidir), onlara kişiselleştirilmemiş ve çocuğa uygun reklam gösterilir.
