Block Trio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Developer: TaraPDF · Contact: kaanyakuphancabukoglu@gmail.com

Block Trio is a puzzle game for all ages: put three blocks of the same color in one tray to clear it. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. We keep it to the minimum the game needs.

What we do not collect

  • No account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, season pass, settings) is stored only on your device.

Age question

When you first open the game we ask for your year of birth. The answer stays on your device and is used only to choose the right kind of ads: players under 13 get child-directed ads, and players under 16 get non-personalized ads. We do not send your year of birth to us.

Data used by services in the app

Game analytics (Google Firebase Analytics). We count game events such as starting or finishing a level, number of moves, boosters used and ads shown. Firebase also records basic device information (model, OS version, language, approximate country from IP address) and an app instance ID. We use this to see which levels are too hard and whether players come back, not to identify you. For players under 13, analytics is limited to what is needed to run the game.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). If the app crashes, a report with the error, device model and OS version is sent so we can fix it.

Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config). The app downloads a few settings (for example ad frequency limits) so we can adjust them without an update. These settings never change level difficulty for individual players.

Ads (Google AdMob). The free version shows ads: none in the first 8 levels, then at most one every few levels, only between levels and never during a level, plus optional ads you choose to watch for rewards. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and information about ad interactions to show and measure ads. For players under 13 ads are child-directed and not personalized; for players under 16 they are not personalized. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland adults are asked for consent first and can change their choice any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. On iOS, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking; we do not ask players under 16. Any purchase removes in-between ads.

Purchases (Google Play / Apple App Store). Coins, booster packs, the season pass, ad removal and the optional monthly club are handled by the store. Prices are the same for everyone. We only receive confirmation of what was bought; we never see your payment details. Parents can require approval for purchases in Google Play Family settings.

Children

Block Trio can be played by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Children receive child-directed, non-personalized ads, and no tracking permission is requested from them.

Your choices

  • Ad consent: Settings → Privacy & ad choices (where available).
  • Advertising ID: reset or limit it in your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all game data stored on your device.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months. To ask about or delete data linked to your device, email us.

Delete your data (Block Trio by TaraPDF)

  1. Game data on your phone (levels, stars, coins, settings, year of birth): uninstall Block Trio, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Block Trio → Storage. It is deleted at once.
  2. Data held by Google for the app (analytics events, crash reports, app instance ID): email kaanyakuphancabukoglu@gmail.com with the subject "Block Trio data deletion". We delete the analytics data linked to your app instance within 30 days and confirm by email.
  3. Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Kept: purchase records stay with Google Play as required by law; we do not hold them. Without a request, analytics data is deleted automatically after 14 months and crash reports after 90 days.

Block Trio — Gizlilik Politikası

Block Trio her yaşa uygun bir bulmaca oyunudur. Hesap ve giriş yoktur; adını, e-postanı ya da telefonunu istemeyiz. Konum, rehber, fotoğraf, kamera ve mikrofon kullanılmaz. İlerlemen (seviyeler, yıldızlar, jeton, güçlendiriciler, sezon yolu, ayarlar) yalnız cihazında saklanır.

Yaş sorusu: İlk açılışta doğum yılın sorulur. Cevap yalnız cihazında kalır ve yalnız reklam türünü seçmek için kullanılır: 13 yaş altına çocuğa yönelik, 16 yaş altına kişiselleştirilmemiş reklam gösterilir. Doğum yılın bize gönderilmez.

Kullanılan servisler: Firebase Analytics (seviye başlama/bitirme, hamle, güçlendirici ve reklam sayıları; cihaz modeli, sürüm, dil, IP'den yaklaşık ülke), Crashlytics (çökme raporları), Remote Config (reklam sıklığı gibi uzaktan ayarlar; seviye zorluğunu kişiye göre değiştirmez), Google AdMob (reklam; reklam kimliği; AB/İngiltere/İsviçre'de yetişkinlere önce onay sorulur, Ayarlar → Gizlilik ve reklam seçimleri'nden değiştirilir), mağaza üzerinden satın alma (fiyatlar herkese aynıdır, ödeme bilgini görmeyiz; ebeveynler Google Play Aile ayarlarından onay zorunlu kılabilir).

Çocuklar oynayabilir; çocuklardan bilerek kişisel bilgi toplamayız, onlara kişiselleştirilmemiş ve çocuğa uygun reklam gösterilir. Uygulamayı kaldırınca cihazdaki oyun verisi silinir. Analiz verisi Google tarafından en çok 14 ay tutulur. Soruların ve silme talebin için: kaanyakuphancabukoglu@gmail.com

Verini silme: Cihazdaki veriler için Block Trio'yu kaldır ya da Ayarlar → Uygulamalar → Block Trio → Depolama'dan verileri temizle. Google'daki analiz ve çökme verileri için "Block Trio data deletion" konusuyla kaanyakuphancabukoglu@gmail.com adresine yaz; 30 gün içinde silinir ve e-postayla onaylanır.
